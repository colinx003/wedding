Theme & attire

The Theme

Pressed Botanical

Inspired by the farm itself — herbarium specimens, wildflowers gone slightly wild, candlelight over long tables. Decor leans into sage, dusty rose, and brass, with parchment linens throughout.

Attire

Garden Formal

Think autumn florals, earth tones, and texture — florals, tweed, and velvet all welcome. Lawn and gravel underfoot, so low heels or flats are kinder to your ankles than stilettos.

The day, roughly

3:30 PM

Guests arrive

Farm parking opens; a shuttle runs from the Rhinebeck lot every fifteen minutes.

4:00 PM

Ceremony

Under the pear trees, roughly thirty minutes, weather permitting — we have a tent on standby just in case.

4:45 PM

Cocktail hour

Cider, small bites, and a lawn game or two on the terrace.

6:00 PM

Dinner & toasts

Family-style dinner in the barn. Keep the toasts warm, not long.

8:30 PM

Dancing

The floor opens, the string lights come on, we dance until midnight.
